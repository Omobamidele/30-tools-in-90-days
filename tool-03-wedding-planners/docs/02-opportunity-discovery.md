# Phase 2 — Opportunity Discovery

Candidates considered, not ranked. Mo selected the first.

| Candidate | Problem | Tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| **Wedding Enquiry Tracker** (selected) | Slow first replies, dropped quote follow-ups, no referral ask | A pure GTM job and a good teaching tool. HoneyBook and Aisle Planner already cover much of it, so the gap is narrow (date capacity, DM and WhatsApp intake). Low ability to pay. |
| Wedding board (vendors, payments, deadlines) | Vendor changes break other things; deposits slip | Real ops value, built in the first prototype. Not a GTM job, and HoneyBook/Aisle Planner cover planning. Dropped from this build. |
| Venue referral tracker | Planners don't know which venues send them couples | Small and useful; left as the learner exercise. |

## Selected system
**Wedding Enquiry Tracker.** One GTM job: capture every enquiry, reply fast, follow up until they book, then turn happy couples into referrals. Renamed from "Wedding Planner OS" under the Field Lab naming rules.

## $30 Tool Test, honestly
A planner who already pays for HoneyBook or Aisle Planner gets lead reminders, auto-responders and timed follow-ups. What those pages don't describe is (a) a capacity check per date across planners and venues before you reply, and (b) a single list for enquiries that arrive in channels without an API. This needs checking with real planners before any claim of a gap is made.
