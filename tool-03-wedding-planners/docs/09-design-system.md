# Design system · Wedding Enquiry Tracker

## Patterns studied and taken
- **Front / Intercom inbox:** a ranked list of people waiting, with the reason and one action. Used for Today.
- **Attio / Pipedrive record panel:** facts, next step and history in a side panel, so you never lose the list. Used for the enquiry panel.
- **Linear:** tabs as pressed buttons, `/` to search, Escape to close.
- **HoneyBook and Aisle Planner (category):** lead stages and lead sources as plain labels.

## Tokens
Brand teal `#0f7a70` (from config) · ink `#1c2830` · muted `#5d6b75` · warm off-white page `#f6f7f5` · status pills: red (late), amber (due), green (done), blue (new), grey (closed). 14 px base, Inter or system UI.

## Rules
No KPI tiles, charts or big figures. Each row leads with the couple's names and an initials avatar. One filled button per row, for the most urgent action; the rest are outlined. Money only appears as a detail.

## Trading-app check
Screenshots of every screen were reviewed against the locked rule: no walls of numbers, no green/red money, no tickers. Pass.
