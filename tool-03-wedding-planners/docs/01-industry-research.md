# Phase 1 — Industry Research: wedding planners

Each claim is marked **Verified** (with source) or **Assumption**. This was a short desk pass for a Standard teaching build, not a market study.

## Business model
- **Assumption.** Small firms (owner plus one to five planners) sell full planning, partial planning and day-of coordination for a fee per wedding. One wedding is months of work, so each lost booking is a large share of a year.

## Customer types and buyers
- **Assumption.** The customer is the couple; the buyer of an internal tool is the owner or lead planner.

## Acquisition channels
- **Assumption, consistent with the Field Lab strategy (section 8, Tool 03).** Instagram, wedding directories, venues' recommended supplier lists and referrals from past couples and other vendors. Enquiries arrive by Instagram DM, WhatsApp, website form and email, often in the evening and at weekends.
- **Verified that the leading tools treat speed and follow-up as the sales problem:** HoneyBook markets lead-form automations with the line "Fast replies win clients" and reminds owners about unanswered leads ([HoneyBook automations](https://www.honeybook.com/product/automations), [inquiry reminders](https://help.honeybook.com/en/articles/9581374-use-inquiry-reminders-in-honeybook-to-follow-up-with-leads)).

## Sales motion and customer journey
Enquiry → first reply → discovery call → quote or proposal → follow-up → contract and deposit → planning → the wedding → thank-you, review and referral.

## Common systems
- **Verified.** HoneyBook: lead and project tracking, proposals, contracts, invoices, automations with triggers, waits and conditions ([help centre](https://help.honeybook.com/en/articles/9519175-honeybook-automations-2-0-triggers-actions-waits-and-conditions), [wedding planners page](https://www.honeybook.com/crm/wedding-planners)).
- **Verified.** Aisle Planner: lead manager, embeddable contact forms, auto-responders, custom booking stages, CSV export, Facebook lead form connection ([lead manager](https://help.aisleplanner.com/en/articles/2210345-adding-leads-into-your-lead-manager), [lead index](https://help.aisleplanner.com/en/articles/6396875-intro-to-your-lead-index), [sales features](https://integration.aisleplanner.com/features/sales)).
- **Assumption.** Many small planners still run on Instagram, WhatsApp, a spreadsheet and a notebook.

## Manual processes and likely bottlenecks
- **Assumption.** DMs and WhatsApp messages have no form to trigger an automation, so they are typed or forwarded in by hand, or never logged.
- **Assumption.** Whether a date is possible depends on how many weddings the team can run that day, which planner is free, and whether the venue is already ours. That check is done from memory.
- **Assumption.** The thank-you and referral ask after the wedding is the step most often skipped.

## Important business events
New enquiry · reply sent · quote sent · nudge due · date booked by someone else · booked · wedding day · day 14 after the wedding.
